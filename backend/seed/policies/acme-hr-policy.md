# Acme Staffing Pvt Ltd — Employee HR Policy (2026)

Version 3.2 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: Human Resources, Noida Sector 62 office

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Acme Staffing Pvt Ltd. Where it is silent, the applicable Indian labour law prevails. Questions about this policy can be raised with the HR team at hr@acme.test or through HR Copilot.

## 1. Working Hours and Attendance

The standard working week is Monday to Friday. Office hours are 09:30 to 18:30 with a one-hour lunch break, which makes an eight-hour working day.

Attendance is recorded through GPS check-in in the HR Copilot app. You must be within 200 metres of the Noida Sector 62 office to check in; check-ins from outside this geofence are rejected. Each employee checks in once per working day and should check out when leaving the office.

If you forget to check in, inform your reporting manager the same day so HR can correct the record. Attendance corrections are only accepted within 3 working days.

## 2. Late Arrival Policy

A check-in after 09:30 is marked as late. If you expect to be more than 30 minutes late, message your reporting manager before 09:30.

Three late marks in a calendar month result in a deduction of half a day of casual leave. Six or more late marks in a calendar month lead to a written warning from HR, and the employee's manager will discuss a punctuality improvement plan. Late marks caused by documented public transport disruptions or extreme weather can be waived by HR on request.

## 3. Leave Policy

Leave is applied for and approved in HR Copilot. All leave is counted in working days (Monday to Friday); weekends that fall within a leave period are not counted. Leave balances reset on 1 January every year.

### 3.1 Casual Leave (CL)

Every employee is entitled to 12 days of Casual Leave per calendar year. Casual leave is meant for personal errands and short absences. It must be applied for at least 2 working days in advance, and no more than 3 consecutive days of casual leave can be taken at a time. Unused casual leave lapses at the end of the year and is not carried forward or encashed.

### 3.2 Sick Leave (SL)

Employees get 8 days of Sick Leave per calendar year. Sick leave can be applied for on the day of absence or retroactively within 2 working days of returning to work. A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry forward.

### 3.3 Earned Leave (EL)

Employees accrue 15 days of Earned Leave per calendar year, credited at 1.25 days per month. Earned leave must be applied for at least 15 days in advance for planned vacations. Up to 30 days of unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next year, and the carried-forward balance is encashed at basic salary when an employee leaves the company.

### 3.4 Other Leave

Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave, as per the Maternity Benefit Act. Paternity leave is 5 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth. Bereavement leave of 3 working days is granted on the death of an immediate family member. Unapproved absence is treated as loss of pay.

## 4. Work From Home (WFH)

Employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week with their reporting manager's approval. WFH requests must be raised by 18:00 on the previous working day.

Client-facing teams (Sales and Recruitment) cannot work from home on Mondays, because Monday is the weekly client review day. Employees on probation are not eligible for WFH during their first 3 months.

On a WFH day you must be reachable on phone and chat between 10:00 and 17:00. GPS check-in applies only to office days; on WFH days your manager records your attendance. Employees who work from home receive an internet allowance of ₹1,000 per month.

## 5. Reimbursement and Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed when they are submitted within 30 days with original receipts or digital invoices. Claims are approved by the reporting manager and paid with the next monthly payroll. Any single claim above ₹10,000 also needs approval from the Finance Manager.

- Local travel for client visits: actual cab fare, or ₹12 per kilometre when using your own vehicle.
- Meals during client visits or business travel: up to ₹500 per day.
- Outstation travel: economy air or AC train, booked through the admin desk at least 7 days in advance.
- Mobile phone bills are reimbursed up to ₹800 per month for Sales and Recruitment staff.

### Acme Wellness Allowance

Every employee can claim an Acme Wellness Allowance of up to ₹6,000 per year for a gym membership, yoga classes or mental health counselling. Submit the receipts as a reimbursement claim under the "Wellness" category.

## 6. Holidays

Acme observes 10 public holidays in 2026: New Year's Day (1 January), Republic Day (26 January), Holi (4 March), Eid al-Fitr (20 March), Independence Day (15 August), Raksha Bandhan (28 August), Gandhi Jayanti (2 October), Dussehra (20 October), Diwali (9 November) and Christmas (25 December).

In addition, each employee may take 2 optional (restricted) holidays per year from the list published by HR in January, for festivals such as Onam, Pongal, Guru Nanak Jayanti or Good Friday. Optional holidays must be applied for one week in advance. If a holiday falls on a weekend, no compensatory day off is given.

## 7. Code of Conduct

Acme is committed to a respectful, inclusive and safe workplace. Harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind is not tolerated. Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee under the POSH Act and can be raised confidentially at ic@acme.test.

Candidate and client data is confidential. Do not share CVs, salary details or client contracts outside the company, and do not store them on personal devices. Company laptops must be locked when unattended.

The dress code is business casual from Monday to Thursday and smart casual on Fridays. Formal wear is expected at client meetings.

Gifts from clients or candidates worth more than ₹2,000 must be declared to HR. Employees must disclose any conflict of interest, such as a relative working for a client. Violations of this code may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Concerns can be reported to hr@acme.test or anonymously through the ethics helpline.
