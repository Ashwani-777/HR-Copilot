# Globex Retail — Store Staff Handbook (2026)

This handbook applies to all staff of the Globex Retail flagship store on MG Road, Bengaluru, and the head office team.

## Shifts and Attendance

The store opens at 10:30. Store staff report by 10:00 for the morning briefing, and a check-in after 10:00 is marked late. Staff check in through the HR Copilot app within 150 metres of the MG Road store. Weekend shifts are rostered by the Store Manager every Thursday.

## Leave

Leave allowances are 12 days of casual leave, 8 days of sick leave and 15 days of earned leave per year. Because weekends are our busiest days, casual leave for a Saturday or Sunday must be approved by the Store Manager at least one week in advance. No leave is approved during the Diwali sale blackout week except for medical emergencies.

## Work From Home

Store staff cannot work from home, because every role on the shop floor needs to be on site. Head office staff (HR, buying and finance) may work from home one day per week, on Wednesdays only, with their manager's approval.

## Uniform and Staff Discount

Store staff wear the Globex uniform: a navy polo shirt with the name badge, black trousers and closed shoes. Two uniform sets are issued every year. All employees get a 20% staff discount on store purchases, up to ₹25,000 per year, on presenting their staff ID at billing.

## Conduct

Be courteous to every customer. Cash counter discrepancies above ₹500 must be reported to the Store Manager the same day. Mobile phones are not to be used on the shop floor during service hours.
